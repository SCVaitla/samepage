# Sales FAQ (internal)

Answers to the questions prospects ask most on discovery and demo calls. Keep answers short
and point prospects to the website for detail. Ask in #sales-help if a question is not here.

## Positioning

**Who is Juniper Desk for?**
Teams of 5 to 500 people who have outgrown spreadsheets and sticky notes but do not want a
heavyweight project management suite. Our sweet spot is agencies, operations teams and
product teams.

**Who do we usually compete with?**
Generic task apps on the low end and enterprise portfolio tools on the high end. Lead with
ease of setup: most teams are running their first project within a day.

## Plans and features

**Can a Starter customer use single sign-on?**
Yes. Starter can enable SSO from the admin settings, so small teams with an identity provider
are covered from day one. Mention this when a prospect is comparing us on security.

**Do we have an API?**
Yes, on Pro and Enterprise. Send technical prospects to the developer docs.

**Can guests be invited?**
Guests are available on Pro and Enterprise. Guests can see only the projects they are
invited to.

## Commercial

**Do we discount?**
Annual billing is already discounted versus monthly. Nonprofits get 25% off. Any other
discount needs approval from the sales director.

**Can prospects pay by invoice?**
Enterprise customers can pay by bank transfer against a purchase order. Starter and Pro pay
by card.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes, 14 days on any plan, no card required.

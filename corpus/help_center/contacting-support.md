# Contacting support

Need help? Here is how to reach us and what to expect.

## Ways to reach us

The fastest way is the in-app chat: choose the question mark in the bottom-left corner and
then Chat with us. You can also email support. We do not offer phone support.

Our support team works round-the-clock, including weekends and holidays, so you can reach a
person whenever you need one, whatever plan you are on.

## Response times

How quickly we reply depends on your plan. Starter workspaces get a first reply within 24
hours, Pro workspaces within eight hours, and Enterprise workspaces within one hour
for urgent issues. Enterprise customers also have a named customer success manager.

## Help us help you

Include your workspace name, what you were trying to do, and what happened instead. A
screenshot or screen recording helps a lot. If something is broken, tell us which browser or
app version you are using.

## Status and outages

Check the status page before contacting us about an outage. During an incident we post
updates there every 30 minutes until it is resolved.

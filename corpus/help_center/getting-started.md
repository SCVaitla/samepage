# Getting started with Juniper Desk

Welcome! This guide walks you through creating a workspace, inviting your team, and
setting up your first project. It takes about ten minutes.

## Create your workspace

Sign up at the Juniper Desk website with your work email, or with Google. You will be asked
to name your workspace; most teams use their company or team name. You can change it later
under Settings, General.

The person who creates the workspace becomes its owner. The owner manages billing and can
make other members admins.

## Invite your teammates

Open Settings, Members and choose Invite. Enter email addresses one per line, or share an
invite link with your team. Invited people get an email and join as members once they accept.

How many people can I invite? On the Starter plan, a workspace can have up to 10 members.
Pro and Enterprise workspaces can have many more. Invited people count toward your limit as
soon as they accept the invitation, and deactivated members do not count.

## Create your first project

Choose New project, pick a template (Blank, Marketing launch, Sprint planning, or Client
onboarding), and give the project a name. Add tasks with the quick-add bar at the top of the
board. Press Enter to add a task and keep typing to add the next one.

## Assign and schedule work

Open a task to set an assignee and a due date, add a description, attach files, and leave
comments. Mention a teammate with @ to notify them. Tasks with due dates appear in each
person's My Work view.

## Next steps

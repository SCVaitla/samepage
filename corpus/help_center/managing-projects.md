# Managing projects and boards

Projects hold your team's tasks. This article explains how to organize them.

## Boards and lists

Every project can be viewed as a board (columns of cards) or a list (rows grouped by
section). Switch views from the top of the project. Changes made in one view appear in the
other straight away.

## Sections and labels

Sections group tasks inside a project, for example To do, Doing and Done, or by week. Labels
are coloured tags you can add to any task and filter by. A task can have several labels but
belongs to one section.

## Filters and saved views

Filter a project by assignee, due date, label or custom field. Save a filter as a view so the
whole team can open it from the project's sidebar.

## Archiving and deleting

Archive a project when the work is done; archived projects are read-only and hidden from the
sidebar but still searchable. Deleted tasks and projects go to the trash, where an admin can
restore them for 30 days.

## Timelines

On the Pro and Enterprise plans, the timeline view shows tasks with start and due dates as
bars. Drag a bar to reschedule, and draw a line between two tasks to make one depend on the
other.

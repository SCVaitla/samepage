# Billing, invoices and refunds

This article explains how billing works, where to find invoices, and when you can get a
refund. Only the workspace owner can see billing settings.

## How billing works

Juniper Desk is billed per seat. You choose monthly or annual billing when you subscribe,
and your subscription renews automatically at the end of each period. Adding seats in the
middle of a period is charged pro rata on your next invoice. Removing seats takes effect at
your next renewal.

## Find your invoices

Go to Settings, Billing, Invoices. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF. To add a VAT
number or change the billing address, choose Edit billing details. Changes apply to future
invoices only.

## Refunds

If Juniper Desk is not right for your team, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of
your first payment or of an annual renewal. Contact support from the in-app chat and include
your workspace name. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive in
five to ten business days.

Requests made after the 14-day window cannot be refunded. If you cancel, your workspace stays
active until the end of the period you already paid for. Monthly renewals are not refundable.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry it three times over seven days and email the workspace owner
each time. After the final retry the workspace becomes read-only until the payment method is
updated. No data is deleted.

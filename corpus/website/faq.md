# Frequently Asked Questions

## Getting started

### What is Juniper Desk?
Juniper Desk is a task and project management tool for teams. Plan projects, assign
tasks, and see who is working on what, on boards, lists and timelines.

### Can I try Juniper Desk before I buy?
Yes. Every plan comes with a free 14-day trial, and you do not need a credit card to start.

### How many people can join a Starter workspace?
A Starter workspace can have up to 10 seats. If your team grows beyond that, you can
upgrade to Pro at any time and keep all of your projects.

## Security and sign-in

### Is single sign-on available on every plan?
Yes. Single sign-on is included on all plans, so your team can log in with the identity
provider you already use.

### Is my data encrypted?
Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We hold a SOC 2 Type II report, which
Enterprise customers can request under NDA.

## Support

### When is support available?
Our support team is available 24/7 by chat and email, on every plan.

### Do you offer phone support?
No. Chat and email are the fastest way to reach us.

## Billing

### Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
Yes. You can change your billing period at your next renewal from Settings, Billing.

### What happens if I cancel?
Your workspace stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. After that,
you can export your data for 30 days before the workspace is closed.

# Pricing

Simple plans that grow with your team. Every plan includes unlimited projects and tasks,
and you can try any plan free for 14 days. No credit card required.

## Starter: $8 per seat per month

Everything a small team needs to get organized.

- Up to 10 seats per workspace
- Unlimited projects, tasks, boards and lists
- 100 MB file attachments
- Google Drive, Slack and email integrations
- 24/7 support by chat and email

## Pro: $15 per seat per month

For growing teams that plan ahead.

- Everything in Starter
- Timelines, dependencies and workload views
- Custom fields and guest access
- Full REST API access
- Faster support response

## Enterprise: let's talk

For organizations that need control, security and scale.

- Everything in Pro
- Admin audit log
- Data residency in the US or EU
- Named customer success manager
- 99.9% uptime commitment

Prices shown are per seat per month, billed annually. Monthly billing is available at a
higher rate. Taxes may apply.
